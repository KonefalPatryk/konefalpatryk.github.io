        I like breaking ideas down into logic and quick prototypes, and I'm always learning: design, narrative, UX and how professional studios approach things, so I can build mechanics efficiently and with the player in mind.

        Quest Log

        1. 2021 – 2024

          BSc (Hons) Computer Games Development First Class

          University of Staffordshire

          Dissertation: Photorealistic Environments with Procedural Content Generation. Modules included Procedural Development & VFX, Prototyping for Technical Designers, Mobile Games Development, Virtual & Augmented Reality and Game Engine Creation.

        2. 2017 – 2020

          UAL Level 3 Extended Diploma, Creative Media Production & Technology (Games Design)

          Northampton College

          Games-focused media course: prototyped games for PC, mobile and controller in Unity with C#, built 3D characters and assets in Maya and Blender, and covered the 3D pipeline from modelling to texturing, rigging and asset prep. Included a large group VR health & safety simulation for the college, game design research and pitching original game ideas.

        3. 2017 – 2018

          Radio Producer

          Inspiration 107.8 FM

          Produced live and pre-recorded shows for an estimated 20–30k daily listeners, including leading the morning show. Planned, scripted, recorded and edited segments in Adobe Audition, presented on air and led the team's technical, IT and editing support.
